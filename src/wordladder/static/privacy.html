Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Word Ladder is a small word game made by Aayushi Rai as a personal project. This page explains what the game stores about you and why.

What we store

  • Your Google account ID and email address, when you sign in with Google. They are used only to sign you in on your devices. Other players never see them.
  • The username you choose. Other players see it in games, invites and on the leaderboard.
  • Your games: the words you play, scores, times, daily results and streaks.
  • A sign-in cookie on each device you use, so you stay signed in.
  • A notification address for each device where you allow notifications, so we can tell you when it's your turn.

We only ask Google for your basic profile and email (the openid, email and profile permissions). We use your Google first name once, to suggest a username, and don't keep it.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone.

Where it's kept

The game runs on Render and its database is hosted by Neon, both in Singapore. Notifications are delivered by your browser's push service (for example Google or Apple).

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from Account → Delete my account in the game. This removes your Google ID, email, sign-ins and notification settings. Finished games remain, shown as played by a deleted player, so your friends' game history still makes sense.
